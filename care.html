Proper Dog Care

Taking care of a dog is an important responsibility. Dogs need proper food, exercise, shelter, medical care, and attention to stay healthy and happy.

1. Provide Healthy Food

Give your dog nutritious food that is appropriate for its age, size, and health needs. Always provide clean drinking water.

2. Give Regular Exercise

Dogs need regular physical activity. Walking, playing, and other safe activities can help keep dogs active and healthy.

3. Keep Dogs Clean

Regular bathing, brushing, nail trimming, and dental care help maintain a dog's cleanliness and comfort.

4. Visit the Veterinarian

Regular veterinary checkups and vaccinations help prevent diseases and keep dogs healthy.

5. Give Love and Attention

Dogs need companionship and affection. Spending time with your dog can help build trust and a strong relationship.
